# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis (SQL Project)

This project performs a detailed analysis of **Netflix’s movie and TV show catalog** using SQL. It focuses on identifying patterns, trends, and insights from the dataset to answer key business questions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the distribution of **movies vs. TV shows**  
- Identify the **most common content ratings**  
- Explore content based on **release year, duration, and country**  
- Categorize content using specific **criteria and keywords**  
- Generate insights to support **content strategy and decision-making**

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset used in this project is sourced from **Kaggle**:

**Dataset Link:** [Movies Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows?resource=download)

---

## 🧠 Key Findings & Insights

### 📌 Content Distribution  
- Netflix hosts a **diverse mix** of movies and TV shows.  
- Movies dominate the catalog, while TV shows have grown steadily in recent years.  

### 📌 Common Ratings  
- Most common ratings reveal the **target audience** for Netflix content.  
- A significant portion of titles fall under family-friendly and young-adult ratings.  

### 📌 Geographical Insights  
- Identified top countries contributing to Netflix’s library.  
- **India** shows strong yearly output, contributing heavily to Netflix’s content base.  

### 📌 Content Categorization  
- Categorizing titles using keywords highlights trends in themes, genres, and content type.  
- Helps understand what kinds of content Netflix prioritizes.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

This SQL-driven analysis provides a clear understanding of:

- What type of content Netflix hosts  
- Which ratings and countries dominate the platform  
- How content varies by release year and duration  
- How keyword-based categorization strengthens strategic insights  

These findings support **content strategy, acquisition planning, and audience-targeting decisions**.
